# 3. Lock zones

Lock zones are used for traffic management to separate areas accessible to AGVs from those for unmanaged vehicles. When free, AGVs can enter and block unmanaged vehicles; when locked by an external vehicle, the zone turns grey and displays blocking and waiting vehicle names. The next vehicle in the queue can lock the zone upon release. Forceful release of external locks or manual bypass of permissions is possible but not recommended due to collision risks.
