# Non-contact safety sensors for collision avoidance

The section describes AGV safety sensors that detect obstacles to slow down or stop the vehicle, protecting personnel and preventing mechanical damage. It details two safety fields: a yellow warning zone that triggers a safe speed reduction and a red emergency zone that causes an immediate stop. Additionally, it notes that ultrasonic scanners assist in locating the environment and calculating distance to obstacles.
