The Qursor platform can be installed with an optional Hector connector service that interacts with external traffic systems via REST API or a dummy system. It defines 2D polygon zones, specifically traffic light and lock zones, to control AGV behavior and visualize zone states.

## `n_61b3edf39545ccd5fe41b5d1` 2. Traffic Light zones

Traffic light zones are used for managing traffic lights, consisting of interdependent yellow and red zones. When an AGV enters the yellow zone, the yellow light activates; upon entering the associated red zone, the yellow light turns off and the red light turns on.

## `n_861784153a718053486374dc` 3. Lock zones

Lock zones are used for traffic management to separate areas accessible to AGVs from those for unmanaged vehicles. When free, AGVs can enter and block unmanaged vehicles; when locked by an external vehicle, the zone turns grey and displays blocking and waiting vehicle names. The next vehicle in the queue can lock the zone upon release. Forceful release of external locks or manual bypass of permissions is possible but not recommended due to collision risks.
